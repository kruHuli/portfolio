# About Kruthik Hulisandra

## who i am

my name is kruthik hulisandra, and yes it's long for a reason. it comes from the moon's position relative to the Krittika constellation at the moment of my birth, rooted in a farming village in Karnataka, India where peacocks and leopards still roam. i moved to america at five. i've been living in the intersection ever since.

that intersection is not a limitation. it's the operating system.

i'm not fully american, not fully indian. not purely technical, not purely business. not quite an athlete, not quite an academic. i've spent my whole life being fluent in multiple worlds without fully belonging to any of them, and it turns out that's exactly the skill set the most interesting problems require.

## what i do

i hold a Master of Business and Science (MBS) from Rutgers University, a dual degree combining an MBA and MS, built specifically for people who refuse to choose between business and technology.

i've led teams, built cultures, and run operations. i started a cricket league at Rutgers Recreation from scratch with two basketball courts and a vision and turned it into a 120-person league running every sunday night. i wrote a leadership manifesto, trained officials, and learned that building culture is harder and more important than any single result.

i think in systems. i communicate across disciplines. i find the pattern underneath the noise faster than most people and i know how to translate it into something actionable.

## how i think

i process the world by observing first and writing second. the writing is where the thinking actually happens, which means my best ideas show up on the page, not before it.

i'm drawn to the intersection of people and systems, culture and technology, individual potential and organizational structure. i studied how Thomas Edison evolved from a solo tinkerer to the architect of the world's first industrial research lab and saw my own leadership journey reflected back at me.

i believe the most valuable person in any room is the one who can talk to everyone in it and then synthesize what they heard into something none of them could have built alone.

that's what i do.

## what drives me

i come from people who sacrificed a lot to give me access to opportunities they never had. that's not a burden i carry. it's a direction i move in.

i'm building toward something specific. the end goal is a formula one pitwall. team principal. the person who holds together hundreds of engineers, drivers, sponsors, and strategists while the car is doing 200mph and every decision has a consequence measured in milliseconds.

that's not a fantasy. it's a career plan. the path runs through building real technical systems in high stakes environments, getting close to the data and strategy side of motorsport, moving into technical leadership, and earning the role over the next decade or so.

everything i'm doing right now is lap one.

i'm only unsuccessful until i'm not.

## outside the resume

i competed in lacrosse, basketball, trivia, and model UN. i have medals in all of them. 

i'm learning guitar (badly, and publicly). i write a Substack where i process what's happening in my life and in the world with more honesty than is probably advisable. i read philosophy for fun and apply it to things that have nothing to do with philosophy.

i've watched someone almost die on the BART and spent forty minutes on a dead-phone train ride thinking about what separates the life i'm building from the one i could have ended up with.

that kind of thinking is what i bring to everything i do.
